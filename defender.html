If you are referring to Land Rover Defender vehicles, the modern lineup includes three primary body sizes along with a special high-performance model

    Defender 90: A compact, two-door, 5-seater SUV ideal for tighter trails.
    Defender 110: A versatile four-door model, typically seating 5 to 7 passengers.
    Defender 130: An extended, four-door model with three rows of seating, accommodating up to 8 passengers.
    Defender OCTA: The ultimate high-performance model with a powerful V8, designed for extreme adventures
